About Indian Law Partners

Legal guidance on matters involving India

Founded in 1999 by Gopika Pant, Indian Law Partners (ILP) is a boutique corporate and commercial law firm with offices in Delhi and Mumbai. Its partners advise Indian and international clients on legal matters involving India, with work spanning cross-border mergers and acquisitions, joint ventures, private equity, banking and finance, regulatory matters, employment, intellectual property, real estate, technology, infrastructure, energy, pharmaceuticals, competition, media, and sports. ILP also collaborates with international law firms on inbound and outbound matters connected with India. Its experience includes India’s first private banking merger, major cross-border financings, infrastructure investments, and greenfield projects.
